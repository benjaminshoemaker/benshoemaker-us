### Quick summary of the broader conversation (March–May 2026)
- **Supportive momentum** is strong among PM trainers, investors, and founders (faster loops, fewer handoffs, new high-paying “builder PM” roles up to $1.4M cited in one post).
- **Pushback** is real from engineers and some designers: quality dilution, “vibe coding” rebrand, need for deep judgment/context that AI can’t fake yet.
- **Company examples** cluster around AI-native teams (Apollo, Superhuman, Anthropic, banks, YC startups) and the “product trio” (PM/design/eng) collapsing into builders.
- No single massive “viral debate thread” dominated the full 3 months, but Gokul’s design post and Sergio’s Product Engineer post generated the most replies/quotes.

The trend is clearly accelerating—hiring managers are actively posting these roles and explaining the “why” (speed + ownership), while experienced builders/engineers are warning that AI multiplies skill, it doesn’t replace it.
